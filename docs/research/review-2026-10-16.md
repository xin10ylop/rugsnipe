# Full review, 2026-10-16: ranked action list

Three independent read-only reviews (strategy and statistics, execution and landing,
operations and safety) were run against HEAD `f9dc2e6` on the day the first Helius-fed
shadow run reached 3,000 attempts. This file merges their findings into one ranked list
with a status per item. Status values: **fixed** (in this branch, verified by the
orchestrator), **in progress** (builder assigned today), **queued** (not started),
**decided** (no code change; the reasoning is recorded), **rejected**.

Context the reviews were given: shadow EV +1.32% per attempt [90% CI +0.87, +1.77]
after 3,091 attempts in 14 h (buyer-behind +4.41%, none −1.50%; filter tertiles
−1.64 / +1.06 / +7.22%; unresolved 8%); backtest OOS +3.2 to +3.7%; the LEAD policy of
docs/04 §2; the two-stage probe of docs/04 §7 and docs/08 §9.

## E. Strategy items decided without code changes

- The shadow EV (+1.32% per attempt, +1.6% per landed trade) is at the low end of the
  backtest range and is consistent with the backtest's structure (tertiles, buyer-behind
  split). Treat the probe as the test of landing rate and real premium, not of the edge.
- Operator arming, mayhem coins, copy-trading, survivor/dip entries, exit refinements,
  graduation momentum, second scalp and hour-of-day stay negative (docs/04 §5); none of the
  reviews found a reason to reopen them.
- Sizing via follower `max_sol_cost` replay (what follower bids say about rank-1 fill
  prices) is a cheap analysis worth doing before stage B; note queued in docs/04 §4.

## What the reviews confirmed as already right

Credential loading (LoadCredential with the ABSENT fallback, permission checks, zeroizing),
the persisted kill latch and its restore order, one durable nonce per intent across all
senders, burn retry with quarantine, positions opened only on our own TradeEvent, the
stage-narrowed caps and ArmSet `probe_stage` match, live refused, `--probe-dry` with
simulateTransaction, the watchdog fed only while the hot loop advances, the eight kill
triggers, unit hardening and CPU pinning, the pre-registered gate rule with the
hour-cluster bootstrap, and the keypair/PEM pre-commit hook.
