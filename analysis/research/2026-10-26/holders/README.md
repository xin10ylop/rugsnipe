# Why the profitable wallets cannot be copied (Oct 26)

Run from the session scratchpad. `pull2.py WALLET [T0]`: a wallet's landed transactions with its token change per mint
in each (sells on any venue are attributed) -> `SP/hold/log/`. The 91 holders: launch buyers (>= 30 launch buys on the
complete tapes) that sell under 20% of their buys inside 13 slots, median buy >= 0.3 SOL.

- `holders.py [IN OUT]`: per wallet, chain-true result per coin (balance change of its transactions on the coin), hold,
  its own price push at entry and exit, the market's move between its buy and its first sell, and two copies: following
  its sells, or selling just before its first sell (a ceiling).
- `moves.py`, `tapemoves.py`: the move by what its buy shows (tip, size, its repeat on the creator), from its own trades
  or from the complete tapes for every wallet.
- `him_parts.py`: the same split for him (key buys on hot operators: mean move +13%; the rest +5%, our cost).
- `rival.py`, `rivalpick.py`: CWiNFA's results and how it picks (3M tip through one relay, j7tracker / 2-4 SOL dev
  buys); chain-true it breaks even (-0.47 SOL on 979 launch buys).
- `check_top.py`: where a copy of the top holders really lands (+11% to +28% over their price: their own sibling wallets
  buy right behind them), their shared coins (three clusters), and creator links.
- `roundtrip.py`: an early version on the out-of-sample finalists (2-6 s holders).
